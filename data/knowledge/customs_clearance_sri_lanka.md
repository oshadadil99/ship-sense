# Customs Clearance SOP - Sri Lanka Imports

## Required documents
Every import shipment needs: the original or telex-released Bill of Lading, the commercial invoice, the packing list, and the Customs Declaration (CusDec) lodged in ASYCUDA.
Food, cosmetics and pharmaceutical imports also need approval from the relevant regulator before clearance.

## HS codes
Every line item on the commercial invoice must have an 8-digit Sri Lankan HS code. A wrong HS code is the most common cause of customs holds.
If the HS code is unclear, the clearing team must request a binding classification before lodging the CusDec.

## Timeline
The CusDec should be lodged at least 2 working days before the vessel arrival (pre-lodgement) so that the container can be cleared within the free time.
Green channel shipments are usually cleared within 1 day. Red channel shipments go to physical examination and usually take 2 to 4 days.

## Customs hold
If a container is placed on customs hold, the customer service team must inform the customer within 4 working hours and open a ticket with the reason code.
