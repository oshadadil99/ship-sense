# Cargo Damage and Loss Claims

## Notice period
The consignee must give written notice of visible damage at the time of delivery, and notice of concealed damage within 3 days of delivery.
Claims for total loss must be filed within 9 months of the expected delivery date.

## Required evidence
A claim must include photos of the damage, the delivery receipt with the damage remark, the commercial invoice, the packing list and a survey report for claims above USD 2,000.

## Liability limit
Unless cargo insurance was purchased, liability is limited to USD 500 per package or per customary freight unit under the Hague-Visby style terms used in our standard contract.
Customers are always advised to buy cargo insurance for high-value shipments.
