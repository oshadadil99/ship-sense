# Shipment Status Codes and Escalation

## Status codes
BKD - booked, container not yet picked up.
GTI - gate in at the port of loading.
LOD - loaded on vessel.
DEP - vessel departed.
TSP - at transshipment port.
ARR - vessel arrived at port of discharge.
DSC - discharged from vessel.
HLD - on hold (customs, documents or payment).
DLV - delivered to consignee.

## Delay definition
A shipment is considered delayed when the current ETA is more than 48 hours later than the original ETA.

## Escalation rules
Delays of more than 72 hours must be escalated to the key account manager.
Any HLD status older than 24 hours must be escalated to the operations manager.
Customers with platinum service level must be proactively notified of every ETA change.
