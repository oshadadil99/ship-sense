# Demurrage and Detention Policy

## Definitions
Demurrage is the charge for a full container that stays inside the port terminal beyond the free time.
Detention is the charge for keeping the carrier's container outside the terminal (at the customer's premises) beyond the free time.

## Free time at Colombo port
Import containers get 5 calendar days of free time at the Colombo terminal, counted from the vessel discharge date.
Export containers get 7 calendar days of free time before the vessel cut-off.
Reefer (refrigerated) containers get only 3 days of free time because of power plug costs.

## Charges after free time
Days 1 to 5 after free time: USD 40 per 20ft container per day and USD 75 per 40ft container per day.
From day 6 onwards: USD 80 per 20ft container per day and USD 150 per 40ft container per day.
Reefer containers have an additional plug-in charge of USD 35 per day.

## Waivers
A demurrage waiver can be requested only if the delay was caused by the carrier or by a customs system outage.
Waiver requests must be submitted to the operations manager within 14 days of the container release, with evidence.
