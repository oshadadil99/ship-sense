# Dangerous Goods (DG) Handling Policy

## Acceptance
Dangerous goods are accepted only with a valid Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) and a signed Dangerous Goods Declaration that follows the IMDG Code.
The UN number, proper shipping name, IMO class and packing group must appear on the booking and on the Bill of Lading.

## Restricted classes
Class 1 (explosives) and Class 7 (radioactive material) are never accepted by ShipSense demo operations.
Class 3 flammable liquids and Class 9 lithium batteries are accepted after DG desk approval.

## Booking lead time
DG bookings must be submitted at least 5 working days before the vessel cut-off, because the carrier must approve the DG stowage.
